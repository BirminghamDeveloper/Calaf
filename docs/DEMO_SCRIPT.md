# Calaf demo script: five minutes

Use the "Calaf Demo" app. Everyone you see is fictional and every reply is scripted. Say so out loud at the start.
If anything goes wrong, Settings, then Reset demo data, puts it back to the start.

## Before you present (one minute)
1. Install the demo APK and open it once so the first launch tour is out of the way, or reset the demo to see it again.
2. Turn on Do Not Disturb. Set the phone to light mode and full brightness.

## Minute 0 to 1: what Calaf is
1. Open the app. The Calaf name animation plays, with the idea credit (Ahmed) and development credit (Mustafa).
2. The three tour screens appear. Say: "Serious intent, privacy first, family can be involved." Tap Next twice, then Get started.
3. On the welcome screen read the three promises: intention, culture, privacy. Mention the 18 and over rule.

## Minute 1 to 2: meet people
1. Tap Try the demo. You are signed in as the demo guest, already set up.
2. On Discover, swipe one card left to pass, one right to like. Show that the round buttons do the same.
3. Tap the right side of a photo to see more photos. Point at the blurred photo if one shows.
4. Open Filters, switch on a filter such as Non smokers, then Show results.

## Minute 2 to 3: matches and compliments
1. Tap Like with a note on a profile and type a kind line. Show the counter (140 characters) and the safety warning if you type a phone number.
2. Go to Matches. Two matches are waiting. Say: "Both people said yes before anyone can message."
3. Open Liked you. Say: "These admirers are simulated in the demo and labelled. In the live app this list is a premium feature."
4. Like one back to get the full screen It's a match moment.

## Minute 3 to 4: chat and family
1. Open the conversation with Abdi. Show the day headings, the Sent and Read marks.
2. Send a message. After a couple of seconds a labelled "Demo reply, simulated" arrives.
3. Open the chat menu. Show Guardian: "The member can let a guardian read this one chat, read only, off by default, and the other person is always told."
4. Show Report and Block, one tap away in the chat header.

## Minute 4 to 5: your profile and safety
1. Profile tab: completion percentage, the photo manager (reorder, main photo, blur) and the approval label on each photo.
2. Settings: Safety, Guidelines, Verification pages. Say: "Verification and premium are simulated and labelled in this demo and never faked in the live app."
3. Log out, then log in with admin and admin. Open the moderation panel on the Profile tab: reports and photo review.
4. Finish on Settings, Reset demo data, so the next person sees a clean start.

## Questions you will be asked
- Is this a real backend? No. The demo runs fully on the phone with no internet permission. The live app uses Firebase and is not deployed yet.
- Are these real people? No. Names, bios and avatars are invented. Photos are illustrated artwork.
- Why no clan field? On purpose. Calaf does not collect or filter on clan or skin colour.
- When is it live? After the live photo pipeline, push notifications, moderation tooling, billing and the privacy work in the plan.
